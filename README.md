# Countinghouse

An open-source finance team for startups. Connect Mercury, Brex, Ramp, Stripe, Gusto, AWS and Carta;
see where every dollar goes, every tax deadline, and your real runway; ask the agent anything in chat.

- **QM** is the agent: a finance-team skill plus tools for your ledger.
- **GBrain** is the memory: vendors, accounts, monthly closes, tax notes and learned rules as plain markdown.
- **Dashboard**: cash, burn, runway, spend by vendor, a tax calendar, and the Delaware franchise tax fix.

Setup instructions land here once the first slice runs.
